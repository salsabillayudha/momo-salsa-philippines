Bandung day trip: the boots mission

Saturday, 5 DecBandung
  • 09:00Sleep in after Colosseum. Grab to Whoosh Halim station (30–45 minutes).
  • 10:00Whoosh Halim → Padalarang (about 30 minutes), then the feeder train to Bandung Station (about 20 minutes, included in the ticket).
  • 11:00Old BandungWalk from the station to Kopi Aroma on Jl. Banceuy, a family coffee roaster running since the 1930s (buy beans to take home). Then Jalan Braga and Jalan Asia Afrika: Dutch-era buildings and Gedung Merdeka, where the 1955 Asia-Africa Conference was held.
  • 12:15LunchBandung street food: Batagor Kingsley (Jl. Veteran) for fried fish dumplings in peanut sauce, or mie kocok (beef noodle soup). Momo should try surabi too if you see a stall.
  • 13:00Boots 1Local brand showrooms in town: Brodo, Portee Goods, Piede, XTURE, Junkard.
  • 15:00Boots 2Cibaduyut, Bandung's shoemaking district. Go for ready-made pairs, since Momo flies the next day.
  • 17:00Oleh-olehKartika Sari on Jl. Kebonjati, near the station, for pisang bolen and other Bandung snacks to take home. Coffee and a sit-down in Braga if there's time.
  • 19:00Feeder to Padalarang → Whoosh back to Halim.
  • ~21:30Back at the hotel, final packing.

Whoosh fares change with demand, roughly Rp150–350k per person one way. If you skip Colosseum, take the 08:00 train instead and add a morning in Dago (cooler hills, cafés with views). Lembang, Kawah Putih and Tangkuban Perahu are too far for this day trip.

Clothes and accessories: factory outlets and local-brand (distro) stores line Jl. Riau (RE Martadinata) and Jl. Trunojoyo, 10 minutes from Braga; swap them in for the brand showrooms if boots are found early. Pasar Baru Trade Center by the station is cheap clothes, fabric and accessories, good for the last hour. Jl. Cihampelas is the old jeans street. Prices at Pasar Baru are for bargaining.

Check opening hours for Kopi Aroma and the food stalls.

food near the hotel

food.jpg

For the open dinner slots (Sat 28, Mon 30, Fri 4) or any lunch. Everything here is within about 15 minutes of The Dharmawangsa. Book ahead for dinner.

Indonesian

  • Plataran Dharmawangsa · Jl. Dharmawangsa Raya, a few minutes from the hotel. Javanese joglo house in a garden, often named Jakarta's best Indonesian restaurant by Tatler. Try the duck curry and sate. Best pick to show Momo Indonesian food.
  • Puja · The Trunojoyo compound, Kebayoran. Indonesian heritage dishes served family style.
  • Remboelan · Senayan City / Plaza Senayan, about 15 minutes. Classic Indonesian dishes in a casual setting, around Rp50–100k per person. Easy lunch on a mall day.
  • Waroeng SS (Spesial Sambal) · Chain from Yogyakarta with dozens of sambals, from mild to very hot. Fun way to test Momo's spice level. Cheap. Check Maps for the nearest branch.
  • Gultik Blok M · Street-side gulai (rice with curried beef) on the corner by Blok M, open at night. Cheap and very Jakarta. Good after drinks.

Pizza and Italian

  • Canella · Hotel Tentrem, Jl. Wijaya, very close. Italian in a hotel setting, quiet and easy.
  • Pizza Marzano · Listed at Jl. Suryo 38, Senopati, and in Senayan City. Casual pizza. Confirm the Senopati address on Maps.
  • Rosso · Shangri-La, Gatot Subroto (about 15 minutes). Often ranked among Jakarta's best pizza.
  • Osteria Gia · Pacific Place SCBD (Monday's dinner) or Plaza Indonesia.

Indian

  • Babooji · Gunawarman, 5 minutes away. Modern Indian (Sunday's dinner).
  • Ganesha Ek Sanskriti / Udupi · Kemang (Thursday's dinner).
  • D' Bollywood · Rasuna Said, Kuningan. Best food reviews of the places I checked.
  • Fez-Kinara / Queen's Tandoor · Kemang. Long-running backups.

Sushi and unagi

  • Man Man Unagi · Senopati. Singapore unagi spot with a Michelin Bib Gourmand there, live eel from Japan grilled over charcoal. Order the hitsumabushi. Expect a queue.
  • Unacho · Plaza Senayan. Charcoal-grilled unagi and hitsumabushi.
  • Unagi Kurofune · Jl. Gunawarman 67, about 5 minutes from the hotel. Japanese unagi chain, open daily 11:30–22:30.
  • Obihiro Nikudon · Blok M. Hokkaido-style grilled pork rice bowls, around Rp55–80k. Try the truffle or kabayaki nikudon. Good cheap lunch for Momo.
  • Shori Sushi Bar · ASHTA District 8, Jl. Senopati. Well-reviewed sushi counter, easy pick near the hotel.
  • Kintaro Sushi · Jl. Suryo, Senopati. More casual, around US$25 per person (estimate).
  • Kiyadon · Pacific Place, SCBD. Long-running casual Japanese, known for its rolls. Good for a quick lunch.
  • Morimoto · The Langham, SCBD. Omakase splurge.
  • Kokuu or Hakuryu · Senopati. Small omakase counters, book well ahead.

Pastry and dessert

  • Braud · Jl. Senopati. Big, flaky croissants. Go early, they sell out.
  • Gontran Cherrier · Senopati. French bakery from Paris, good croissants and tarts.
  • Butterman · Jl. Cikatomas, Kebayoran. Almond croissants, chocolate or marzipan.
  • Moon Gypsy · Jl. Senopati. Croissants, egg tarts and éclairs.

Something special

  • August · Jl. Senopati 39. On Asia's 50 Best Restaurants list and named best restaurant in Indonesia. Tasting menu, book well ahead. Good for the farewell dinner.
  • Turkuaz · Gunawarman. Turkish, generous and fun to share.
  • Le Quartier · Gunawarman. French brasserie, good for a long dinner.

Boots mission notes

Before Saturday

When trying them on

  • Try on in the afternoon, when feet are slightly swollen, for a truer size.
  • A little heel lift is normal in new leather boots; it eases as they break in.
  • Ask whether it's real or synthetic leather, and whether the sole can be replaced.
  • Keep the receipt and ask about size exchanges before the 19:00 train.
Times are estimates. Check Whoosh fares and schedules, museum, shop and restaurant hours the day before (book Indian restaurants for weekend nights).
